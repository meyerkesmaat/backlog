This device is connected.

Your log is kept in a secret gist on your GitHub account. Changes are sent up a few seconds after you make them, and other devices pick them up when you open the app there.

To connect another device, open the app there, go to Data → Sync between devices and paste the same token.

Keep your phone and computer in step. Your log is stored in a secret gist on your GitHub account, and every device you connect syncs with it automatically.

  1. Open this GitHub page. It's already set up with only the gist permission ticked.
  2. Pick an Expiration (“No expiration” means you never have to redo this), scroll down and click Generate token.
  3. Copy the token (it starts with ghp_) and paste it below. Also keep it in a note, so you can paste it on your other devices.

The token can only read and write your gists. It's stored on this device only.
